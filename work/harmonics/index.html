Digital Product Overhaul · Brand Strategy · Executive Operations

Stanford Harmonics

Re-architecting the digital presence, visual identity, and organizational operations of Stanford’s premier rock & pop a cappella group across four years of executive leadership.

Roles Held Publicity Director (4 yrs), President (Soph.), Album Manager (Sen.)
Timeline 2022–2026 (4 Academic Years)
Web Growth Impact +75% Avg. Monthly Traffic · +100% Peak Audition Traffic
Deliverables Full Web Redesign, 6 Show Campaigns, 3 Audition Cycles, 1 Studio Album
01 · Problem & Users

The First-Impression Bottleneck

At Stanford, the collegiate a cappella scene is fiercely competitive, and every September the group’s website serves as the primary conversion funnel for incoming students deciding where to audition.

However, the legacy stanfordharmonics.com site—built on an outdated 2014 Squarespace 7.0 template—was fragmented and difficult to navigate. Content was scattered across redundant pages with outdated member rosters, broken or missing streaming embeds, and a sparse, isolated videos page. Prospective auditionees and booking clients had to click through multiple dead-end menus just to hear what the group sounded like or find upcoming live performances.

02 · Digital Product

Information Architecture & Redesign

Taking solo ownership of the redesign, I audited how prospective auditionees, concert-goers, and booking clients navigated the site, then overhauled both the information hierarchy and visual aesthetic:

Consolidating the Media Funnel: Merged the fragmented “Listen,” “Albums,” and sparsely populated “Videos” pages into a single, high-impact Music hub. Added native streaming embeds alongside music videos and introduced a dedicated #accolades anchor section to immediately surface the group’s national awards and CARA recognition.

Building the Shows Archive & Lead Capture: Created a dedicated Shows page uniting upcoming concert ticket details with an archive of past show posters and direct links to full YouTube performance playlists. Replaced the static contact page with an actionable, structured inquiry form to streamline gig bookings and audition questions.

Designing for Non-Technical Handoff: Rather than building a custom-coded site that would decay once I graduated, I intentionally architected the new system on modern Squarespace—establishing reusable page templates and clear layout guardrails so future student board members without technical backgrounds can effortlessly maintain rosters, shows, and releases.

Measurable Growth Outcome

Following the information architecture and visual overhaul, average monthly website traffic increased by 75% (from ~200 to ~350 monthly visitors), and peak September recruitment traffic doubled by 100% (jumping from ~600 to ~1,200 visitors during audition season).

03 · Brand System

4 Years as Publicity Director

Serving as Publicity Director across all four years at Stanford, I owned the group’s end-to-end visual identity, recruitment marketing, and live-event collateral:

Concert Campaigns & Print Collateral: Designed 6 full concert campaigns, including large-format campus posters, digital promotional assets, and multi-page printed show programs.

Audition Recruitment & Annual Merch: Directed multimedia promotional campaigns across 3 fall audition cycles to drive freshman turnout, and designed 4 generations of custom member apparel—creating a cohesive visual culture both on and off stage.

04 · Operations & Production

President & Album Manager

Beyond design and digital product ownership, I held two of the organization’s core operational and technical execution roles:

Business Manager / President (Sophomore Year): Directed all organizational operations, financial strategy, budgeting, audio equipment procurement, tour and performance logistics, internal knowledge management, and external university communications.

Album Manager, Lead Recording Engineer & Arranger (Senior Year): Spearheaded end-to-end production for the group’s upcoming studio album, Ashes to Ashes (releasing later in 2026). I served as lead recording engineer capturing vocal tracks, owned the master production timeline and scheduling, and managed the external vendor pipeline across vocal editors, mixing and mastering engineers, licensing partners, album cover artists, and streaming distributors—while also arranging Toby Fox’s “BIG SHOT” for the record.
