group communication · XR · research · telecommunication

honors thesis

Led a 12-month study at the Stanford Virtual Human Interaction Lab testing the impact of spatiality and virtual environments on group communication. Recruited 143 participants and ran their sessions, trained seven research assistants, and wrote Python and R scripts to process multimodal data. We found that immersion does not necessarily improve interpersonal attention.
