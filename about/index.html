About Me

Hi, I’m Violet.

I recently graduated from Stanford University with a B.S. in Symbolic Systems, concentrating in Human-Computer Interaction, with Departmental Honors.

I got my start in graphic design and have always been interested in branding, technology, and how people interact with both. Symbolic Systems was a natural fit for me at Stanford because it combines psychology, computer science, and design—focusing not just on how to build software, but on why people choose certain tools and how they actually use them.

Across my classes, research, and campus groups, I’ve usually ended up in roles that connect different parts of a project together. Depending on the day, that has meant running 150 participants through an Apple Vision Pro study and writing Python scripts to make sense of the data, building React Native apps with small teams, or managing budgets, schedules, and recording sessions for my a cappella group.
