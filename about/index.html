About Me · Symbolic Systems (HCI)

Designing tools that feel effortless.

I’m Violet Crow, a recent graduate from Stanford University with a B.S. in Symbolic Systems (concentrating in Human-Computer Interaction) and Departmental Honors.

My path into product started with a background in graphic design and a fascination with branding, technology, and human behavior. At Stanford, I gravitated toward Symbolic Systems because it sits directly at the crossroads of cognitive psychology, computer science, and design—answering not just how to build complex systems, but why people choose the tools they do and how they actually use them.

I thrive as a connective tissue across disciplines. Whether I’m writing Python pipelines to analyze multi-modal XR telemetry, building React Native and Supabase features alongside engineers, or managing budgets, vendors, and operations for large-scale campus organizations, I love translating ambiguous user needs into functional, elegant products.
