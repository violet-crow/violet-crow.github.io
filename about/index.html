About Me

I recently graduated with honors from Stanford University, where I studied Symbolic Systems with a concentration in Human-Computer Interaction.

My interest in products started with graphic design. According to my mom, I could recognize Dunkin' Donuts stores from their logo (and ask for a donut, of course) before I could read. I have always cared about how things look, how they're organized, and how people make sense of them. At Stanford, I chose Symbolic Systems because it let me combine my love of design with computer science and cognitive psychology, learning how to build software to match how people think and behave.

Whether I’m working on a research study, a class project, or a student organization, I usually gravitate toward roles that hold things together. I love talking to users, figuring out what to build, writing code to connect the pieces, and collaborating to keep everything on track.

Honors Research Lead

Study Design, Operations & Data Analysis
